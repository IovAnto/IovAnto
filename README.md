Most of what I write is systems software: small tools that live in the terminal and sit
close to the operating system, almost always on Linux. I like knowing what happens
underneath, and I notice when a program burns more than it should.

I finished my degree in July 2026 with a thesis on optimising Vision-Language-Model Action
pipelines, after an internship at the university's Computer Science department working on
how well VLM agents reason about indoor navigation.
